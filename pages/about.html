Company Profile

Who We Are

BharatNexgen Automation Pvt. Ltd. is an AI-first technology company incubated at the Technology Business Incubation Foundation (TBIF), IIT Ropar, Punjab, India. We are building the next generation of intelligent automation systems that solve real-world challenges across India's most critical sectors.

Our interdisciplinary team combines deep expertise in machine learning, computer vision, IoT, and domain-specific knowledge in defence, healthcare, agriculture, industrial quality control, and education.

We build production-grade, edge-deployable AI systems that are fast, accurate, and accessible — delivering real value to government bodies, PSUs, private enterprises, and research institutions across India.

Why We Exist

Motivation & Goals

🎯

Our Mission

To develop and deploy accessible, high-accuracy AI & computer vision systems that solve India's most pressing challenges in defence, health, agriculture, industry, and education — making intelligent automation a reality for every sector.

🔭

Our Vision

To become India's foremost AI automation partner, creating a future where intelligent systems empower government institutions, PSUs, private enterprises, and research organizations to achieve more with precision and efficiency.

💡

The Problem We Solve

Manual, error-prone processes in pharma QC, border surveillance, institutional attendance, and crop monitoring waste billions annually. We replace these with fast, reliable, edge-deployable AI that works on low-power hardware in real-world conditions.

🚀

Our Approach

We combine cutting-edge deep learning with domain expertise, build real prototypes with production-quality code, train on carefully curated Indian datasets, and partner with IIT faculty to ensure scientific rigour in every solution we ship.
